about

verifieds only lists roblox accounts that currently hold the verified badge, rechecked around the clock straight from the roblox api

new badge holders are found automatically by crawling friends of known verified accounts and cross checking public verified lists, since roblox offers no way to list them all directly

accounts that lose the badge or get terminated are removed, every change lands in the feed, and the page updates itself as it happens
